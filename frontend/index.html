1. Upload Dataset

3. Select Target / Label

Choose the column the model should predict. The remaining usable columns become model features. Classification targets with at least two and at most 100 classes are supported.

Viva Summary

Dataset: supplied through the dashboard. Target: selected by the user. Gradient clipping: limits each sample's gradient contribution. Noise: adds calibrated randomness to the aggregate update. Epsilon: reports privacy loss under the configured Opacus accountant and delta. Kubernetes: provides orchestration/scaling and is separate from the DP guarantee.
